About HipHopLord

HipHopLord is a curated directory built for one purpose: helping independent hip hop artists find the people and spaces that actually understand the sound they're chasing — without wading through generic freelance marketplaces or guessing who's legitimate.

We organize listings across four core categories:

  • Producers & Beatmakers
  • Mixing & Mastering Engineers
  • Cover Art & Visual Designers
  • Recording Studios & Spaces

Every listing is tagged by sub-genre, city, and price range so artists can filter down to exactly what they need — a boom bap sample flip, a trap mixing engineer, a cover art designer who works in 3D, a vocal booth in Atlanta — instead of scrolling through everything.

Listings are sourced from public profiles on SoundCloud, Fiverr, and Google Maps, then filtered and organized for relevance. If you spot a listing that's outdated or want to be added, reach out on the Contact page.
