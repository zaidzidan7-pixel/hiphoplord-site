Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: August 19, 2026

In Plain Terms

Some of the outbound links on HipHopLord Directory are affiliate links. If you click one of those links and go on to purchase a service, we may earn a small commission from the platform — at no extra cost to you. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or go there directly.

Which Listings Involve Affiliate Links

Specifically, this applies to listings in the Mixing & Mastering Engineers and Cover Art & Visual Designers categories, which link out to Fiverr through our affiliate account. Any card or page whose outbound link carries the label "View on Fiverr" is an affiliate link (technically marked with rel="sponsored" in the page's HTML, per Google's own guidance for paid/affiliate links).

Producers & Beatmakers listings link out to SoundCloud, and Recording Studios & Spaces listings link out to each studio's own website or Google listing — neither of those categories are paid or affiliate placements.

Editorial Independence

Whether or not a listing carries an affiliate link has no bearing on how it's described, rated, or ranked. Ratings, review counts, and every fact shown on a listing card or page are pulled directly from the provider's real public profile (Fiverr, SoundCloud, or Google) — we do not accept payment to feature, rank, or favorably describe any listing.

Why We're Telling You This

We believe you have a right to know when money may change hands as a result of your click. This disclosure exists to comply with FTC guidance on affiliate marketing and to be transparent about how this directory sustains itself.
